About

My interests lie at the intersection of natural language processing, language model evaluation, and trustworthy AI. In particular, I am interested in questions that leave important interpretive conditions unspecified and in understanding what model representations can tell us about those missing conditions.

Alongside my studies, I work on data platform operations at the National Cancer Center, with practical interests in monitoring, infrastructure reliability, and AI-assisted systems.

Research interests

Topics I currently explore include:

Question underspecification

Identifying when a question lacks the context needed for a well-defined answer.

Language model evaluation

Assessing model behavior beyond aggregate accuracy and benchmark scores.

Model representations

Studying what hidden-state features reveal about language understanding.

Reliable AI systems

Bringing evaluation, observability, and dependable operations together.
